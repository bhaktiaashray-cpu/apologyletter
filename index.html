💌 To My Dearest Shubh

My Dearest Shubh,

I'm sorry... truly, deeply, and with all my heart.

Sometimes we hurt the people we love the most, not because our love is small, but because we fail to show it in the way they deserve. If my words, my actions, or my silence ever became the reason behind your tears, I carry that regret with me every single day.

You are one of the most beautiful parts of my life. You are the smile that brightens my darkest days, the peace my restless heart searches for, and the person who has unknowingly taught me what unconditional love feels like.

Every laugh we shared, every little conversation, every memory with you is a treasure that I never want to lose. The thought of losing you hurts far more than any mistake I've ever made.

I cannot change yesterday, but I can promise you something about tomorrow. I promise to listen before reacting, to understand before judging, to choose patience over pride, and to love you a little more with every sunrise.

I don't expect forgiveness just because I said "I'm sorry." I know trust is rebuilt through actions, not words. But I hope these words help you see that my heart has never stopped choosing you.

If life gave me a thousand chances, I would choose you in every single one of them. I would still fall in love with your smile, your kindness, your stubborn little moments, and every imperfect thing that makes you... you.

So today, I'm not asking for perfection. I'm only asking for one more chance... One more conversation. One more smile. One more opportunity to hold your heart a little more carefully than before.

Because my happiest place has always been beside you. And if you'll let me... I'd love to spend every tomorrow proving that this apology comes from the deepest corner of my soul.

Will you forgive me, my darling?

❤️ Forever Yours,
With all my love.
